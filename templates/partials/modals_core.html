Welcome to the GCM Platform

Your compliance command centre for 205 markets. Everything runs locally and offline.

What you can do
  • Matrix – pick a product, see which of 205 markets need in-country testing, documents or a self-declaration, and export it to Excel.
  • Map – heatmaps for readiness, testing barriers, lead time, environmental rules and active alerts; click any country for its dossier.
  • Alerts – every regulation change explained in plain English, with who is affected, what to do and by when. Triage them, ask follow-up questions, turn them into actions.
  • Horizon & Risk – a single timeline of every deadline and certificate expiry, and a Compliance Risk Index per product and region.
  • Portfolio – products, certificates and market readiness in one place.
  • Document Audit – point at a folder of test reports and declarations; get a health score, what must be re-tested or re-signed, gaps per market and an Excel directive.

Good to know
  • The ledger of regulatory changes is tamper-evident (hash-chained). Its verification status is shown in the ledger dialog.

  • Explanations work without internet. Adding an Anthropic API key in Settings enables Claude-enhanced briefs.
