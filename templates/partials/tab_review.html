How this works
  • Surveillance scans put detected notices in this queue. Approving one writes the ledger entry and creates the alert; rejecting one records why, and the same notice is never queued again.
  • Proposed changes (from the matrix or a country fact sheet) become edited & approved overrides on top of the shipped data when approved, and can be reverted to the shipped value.
  • Source links pre-filled in the matrix are authority portals (starting points), not citations of a specific rule. A record shows Verified only when a reviewer has recorded it with a citation URL; it needs re-verification after 12 months.
  • Limitation: the reviewer is a self-declared name (Settings). This single-user tool has no logins, so the audit trail shows what was decided and proves it was not altered afterwards, but it cannot prove who typed the name. You can require a different name for approver and proposer.
